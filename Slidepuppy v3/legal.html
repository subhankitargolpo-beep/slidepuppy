Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2025

Welcome to SlidePuppy! Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our web-based application. Our core privacy principle is transparency: SlidePuppy is designed to be a client-side application, meaning your sensitive data stays in your browser.

1. Information We Collect and Why

SlidePuppy operates almost entirely within your web browser. We do not have our own servers to store your personal data. Here’s a breakdown of the data involved:

  • API Keys (Gemini and Google Search): You are required to provide your own API keys to use the service. These keys are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. They are never transmitted to, or stored on, any server owned by SlidePuppy. Your browser sends these keys directly to the respective Google services for authentication.
  • Presentation Content: Any information you enter into the form fields (such as topic, difficulty, audience, custom slide instructions) is used to construct prompts. This data is processed in-memory during your session and sent directly to the Google Gemini API to generate your presentation. We do not log, store, or analyze this content.
  • Uploaded Images: Images you upload are converted to a Base64 format and stored in your browser's memory for the current session. This data is used solely to embed the images into the generated PowerPoint file.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to provide the core functionality of SlidePuppy:

  • Your API Keys are used to authenticate you with Google's services.
  • Your Presentation Content is sent to the Gemini API to generate slide content and to the Google Search API for research purposes.
  • Your Uploaded and Searched Images are embedded into the final downloadable presentation file (`.pptx`).

3. Third-Party Services

SlidePuppy is an interface that relies on third-party services to function. Your interaction with these services is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AI (Gemini): Presentation content is sent to the Gemini API. We recommend you review Google's Privacy Policy to understand how they handle data submitted to their services.
  • Google Programmable Search Engine: If you enable the custom search feature, your search queries are sent to the Google Search API. This is also covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

We do not operate servers that store your personal data.

  • Local Storage: The only information we persist is your API keys and form preferences, which are saved in your browser's localStorage for your convenience. You have full control over this and can clear your browser's storage at any time to remove this data permanently.
  • In-Memory Processing: All other data, such as presentation topics and generated content, exists only for the duration of your active session in the browser. Once you close the browser tab, this information is gone.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your data. You can view, edit, or clear your saved API keys and preferences by using your browser's developer tools to inspect and clear `localStorage` for this site.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
